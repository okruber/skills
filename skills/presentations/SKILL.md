# Presentations

Olle's presentations primer, developed over 25 years as a lawyer, banker and
operator.

Great presentations communicate insight, not just information. The objective
is to help the audience make better decisions.

## When this applies

This skill applies when the deliverable is a sequence of slides that someone
will present or read as a deck. The words "presentation", "deck", "slides", or
"pitch" in the request are the signal.

This skill does not apply when Olle asks for an HTML version of a note, a page,
a visual, a diagram, or a document. HTML is a format, not a presentation. If the
request is ambiguous, build what was asked and do not impose deck structure.

## The rules

1. Lead with the conclusion.
2. Every slide communicates one idea.
3. Slide titles state the conclusion, not the topic.
4. Present analysis, not data dumps.
5. Every chart has a clear takeaway.
6. End with a recommendation and the decision required.

## The test

If someone reads only the slide titles, they should understand the story. If
the audience has to figure out your point, the presentation is bad.

## How to build a deck

1. Write the storyline first: the slide titles only, in order, before any slide
   body.
2. Run the test on that title list. Rewrite until the titles alone carry the
   argument.
3. Build each slide body as the evidence for its title. Cut any content that
   does not support the title, or move it to an appendix.
4. Make the last slide the recommendation. It names the decision required, who
   makes it, and by when.

## Slide titles

A slide title is a full sentence that states the takeaway. It includes the
number when there is one.

| Topic title (wrong) | Conclusion title (right) |
| --- | --- |
| Executive summary | Q3 cloud spend rose 38%, and a 45% BigQuery cut can reverse most of it |
| What changed in Q3 | BigQuery alone drove the increase, while compute stayed flat |
| Budget options | Optimizing costs a slot commitment but saves at least 216k EUR a year |
| Next steps | Approve the optimization plan by Oct 15 to lock in Q4 savings |

## Charts and data

Every chart states its takeaway in the slide title or a callout on the chart.
A table of raw numbers is a data dump. Replace it with the comparison or trend
that matters, and highlight the figure the title refers to.

## Delivering the deck

Place the title-only storyline, as a numbered list, above the slides. This lets
Olle run the test at a glance.

## Reviewing an existing deck

Extract the titles into a list and run the test. Report each slide that breaks
a rule, name the rule, and propose a conclusion title for it.
